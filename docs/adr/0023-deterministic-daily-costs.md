# ADR-0023: Deterministic food and local transport costs

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-28

## Context

Daily costs are often fabricated as a percentage of hotel price or generated by an LLM. Neither
method is reproducible or tied to source evidence.

## Decision

Food is calculated from a versioned, alcohol-free fixed basket. Economy and standard formulas and
age factors are deterministic; comfort remains missing until calibrated. Effective spend days equal
the scenario nights because arrival and departure count as half-days.

Local transport uses sourced official fares. The configured rides per day are compared with day and
weekly passes, and the cheapest applicable public option is used. Child fares require explicit
age-based evidence. Missing child fare evidence makes the component missing rather than zero. Taxi
and airport transfers are excluded.

Provider adapters and destination datasets remain separate later slices. They may supply evidence
but cannot change these formulas.
